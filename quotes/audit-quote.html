Scope of Work

  • Dispatch duplication fix: Investigate and fix dispatches being recorded twice (e.g. two breakfast dispatches); confirm stock levels are unaffected and clean up the existing duplicate records.
  • VAT handling and purchases reconciliation: Establish whether VAT affects the Purchases figures that differ between the physical report and the system report; add support for VAT-free items; correct VAT calculations in exported data.
  • Invoice OCR into Purchase Order: Read an uploaded proof/invoice using OCR and pre-fill the Purchase Order lines for review before saving.
  • Negative stock and item pricing across facilities: Find why opening and closing stock go negative; resolve unit prices changing over time and prices not being shared between facilities through the item-facility link.
  • Report accuracy and facility-specific reports: Correct the output/input figures; allow reports for a specific facility; stop items appearing in a facility's exported report when they were never bought, received or dispatched there.
  • Closing value from weekly bin count: Set the closing stock value from the Friday bin count, since counts are done once a week.
  • Receiving: supply source: Let the person receiving stock record where the supply is coming from.
  • Dispatch precision and decimal input: Allow dispatching the full remaining quantity (e.g. 0.5 of 0.5 available, leaving stock at exactly 0); fix decimal entry for amounts under 1 kg where the dot is not accepted.
